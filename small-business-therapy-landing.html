FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about Small Business Therapy.

Is this really free? +
Yes — completely free, forever. No sign-up, no credit card, no catch. It's a free resource built by Maximum Marketing Solutions to help Australian small business owners. The only ask is that if you find value in it, you consider reaching out to MMS when you're ready for professional marketing help.
What's the difference between the Learning Centre and Premium? +
They solve different problems. The free app is for "what do I do about this specific crisis right now" — fast, tactical, step-by-step. The Learning Centre is for "I want to actually get good at this" — 18 structured modules on strategy, profit and growth, bought once for lifetime access, with no ongoing subscription. Premium is an ongoing relationship layered on top of the free app — personalised AI advice, templates and priority support for a monthly fee. Many business owners use all three: the free app for crises, the Learning Centre to build real skills, and Premium for ongoing support in between.
Do I need Premium to use the Learning Centre, or vice versa? +
No — they're independent. You can buy a single Learning Centre module, the full bundle, subscribe to Premium, or do any combination, in any order. Nothing in the free app requires either purchase, and neither paid product requires the other.
Who is this built for? +
Small and medium business owners in Australia — particularly in trades, services, retail and industrial sectors. The advice is practical and Australian-specific, referencing the ATO, QCAT, Fair Work and other local resources.
How do the strategy notes work? +
Every action point in every plan has a "+ Note" button. Click it and type your own thoughts, decisions and next steps for that specific point. Your notes are saved privately on your device and automatically build into a strategy document under the Strategy tab. You can print it or copy it to share with your accountant or advisor.
Can I use it on my phone? +
Yes — it's fully mobile-optimised. Open it in your phone browser and you can add it to your home screen for quick access. It works on iPhone and Android, and on desktop computers and laptops as well.
Is my data private? +
Your strategy notes are saved only on your device — they're not sent to any server or seen by anyone at MMS. The only data we collect is if you fill in the contact form to request a consult, or purchase a Learning Centre module or Premium subscription.
I want this for my clients — can I licence it? +
Yes — we offer white-label versions for marketing agencies, business coaches, accountants and banks who want their own branded version for their client base. Get in touch to discuss pricing and options.
Does it replace a business advisor? +
No — and it's not designed to. It's a first-response tool for when you need to act fast and don't know where to start. Think of it like a first aid kit — it stabilises the situation. For deep, ongoing strategy, working with a real advisor is always better. That's why every plan includes a free consult offer from MMS.
What's the difference between Free and Premium? +
The free version gives you everything you need to get started — all 14 crisis categories, full step-by-step action plans, how-to guides on every action point, personal strategy notes and printing. It's genuinely useful and completely free forever with no strings attached. Premium takes it further — you get AI-powered advice personalised to your specific business situation, downloadable templates (cash flow forecasters, proposal scripts, staff agreements), a monthly Business Health Check, the ability to email your strategy directly to your accountant, priority access to the MMS team for quick questions, and exclusive resources to support your ongoing business journey. Premium is $9.99/month or $79/year.
When is Premium launching? +
Premium is currently in development and launching soon. Register your interest using the button in the Pricing section and we'll notify you the moment it goes live — along with an early bird discount for those who register before launch. Your details go directly to the Maximum Marketing Solutions team.
Do I need to pay anything to use the app right now? +
Absolutely not. The core platform — everything currently in the app — is free forever. No credit card required, no trial period, no hidden fees. You can use every action plan, every how-to guide, write all your strategy notes and print your strategy document without spending a cent. Premium and the Learning Centre are optional upgrades for business owners who want to go deeper.
What templates and resources come with Premium? +
Premium subscribers will get access to a growing library of practical business resources including: a 13-week cash flow forecaster, a new client proposal template, a cold calling script builder, a staff performance review template, a business health scorecard, a Google Ads budget calculator, a break-even analysis tool, and monthly new resources added based on subscriber feedback. Every resource is designed for Australian small businesses — not generic American templates.
Can I cancel Premium at any time? +
Yes — cancel anytime with no lock-in, no cancellation fees and no awkward phone calls. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your billing period and then revert to the free version. Your notes and saved plans are never deleted regardless of your subscription status. Learning Centre modules are a one-off purchase with lifetime access, so they're never affected by your Premium status either way.
How does the AI advice in Premium work? +
Rather than fixed action plans, Premium subscribers can describe their specific situation — their industry, location, revenue, the exact problem they're facing — and receive personalised advice tailored to their business. Think of it as having a business advisor available 24/7 who understands the Australian market, knows your industry, and gives you straight answers without the consulting fee. It's powered by leading AI technology and guided by the real-world business experience of the Maximum Marketing Solutions team.
