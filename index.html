About Small Business Therapy

A free resource by Maximum Marketing Solutions to help Queensland small business owners navigate tough times — with honest, practical advice and a personal strategy builder.

📲 Add this to your home screen

Install Small Business Therapy like an app — it opens full-screen, works offline once loaded, and there's nothing to download from an app store.

iPhone (Safari): tap the Share icon, then "Add to Home Screen."
Android (Chrome): tap the menu (⋮), then "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App."

Who made this?

Maximum Marketing Solutions is a Brisbane-based SEO, Marketing and Sales Strategy agency specialising in helping small and medium businesses grow across Brisbane, Gold Coast and Sunshine Coast.

Is this free?

Yes — completely free, no signup required. We built it because we work with small business owners every day and know how isolating it feels when things go wrong.

How do the notes work?

Every action point has a note button. Click it, type your thoughts, save it. All your notes build up into a strategy document under the Strategy tab — which you can print or copy at any time. Notes are saved privately on your device.

Want personalised advice?

Every business situation is different. Reach out for a free 30-minute consult — no hard sell, just honest advice tailored to your situation.
